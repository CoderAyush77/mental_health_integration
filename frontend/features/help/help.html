Important Safety & Medical Disclaimer

SereneMind is NOT a medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, or therapeutic service. The breathing exercises, guided meditations, journal suggestions, and AI chatbot prompts are designed solely for educational, self-reflection, and general mindfulness purposes. They do not constitute, nor are they a replacement for, clinical advice, professional therapy, psychiatric counseling, or medical diagnosis.

Do not ignore or delay professional medical assistance because of information read, suggested, or generated in this application. If you are experiencing a severe mental health crisis, self-harming thoughts, or a psychiatric emergency, please reach out to qualified professionals immediately.

Working with SereneMind

🍃

Breathing Exercises

Access the breathing card, select a session duration, and sync your inhalations and exhalations with the animated breathing ring. Designed to help ground your attention and lower stress.

🌿

Guided Meditation

Pick a calming soundscape (such as Morning Serenity, Ocean Waves, or Zen Melodies), select your timer, and let ambient audio sweep away mental strain.

💬

AI Chat Companion

Chat anonymously and privately with SereneBot. Express your thoughts, work through emotional blocks, or trigger custom breathing routines whenever you need support.

📖

Mindfulness Journal

Maintain a personal reflection journal. Write down your feelings and keep a clean, searchable history of your daily milestones and reflections.

📊

Mood Analytics

Track your mental health over time. Review mood trends, weekly averages, and keep your daily streak alive to stay motivated on your mindfulness journey.

⚙️

Preferences & Theme

Customize settings to match your mood. Configure custom email check-in frequencies, customize profile info, or toggle between light and dark themes.
