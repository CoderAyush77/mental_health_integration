Data Privacy & Agreement

To protect your mental wellness journey and keep your details secure, please review and accept our data security standards.

1. Zero Plaintext Leakage

SereneMind is built with privacy at its core. All written logs, mood tracker variables, and custom notes are stored locally in your browser's sandboxed environment or processed through fully encrypted pipelines. No plaintext information is transmitted to external database systems.

2. End-to-End Cryptographic Security

When syncing data or requesting conceptual LLM assistance, personal identifiers are obfuscated and payload data is encrypted client-side using industry-standard advanced cryptography before hitting the server. Only you hold the decryption authority.

3. Absolute Ownership & No Ad Tracking

We believe your mental health reflections are sacred. SereneMind enforces a strict zero-data-monetization policy. Your activity, mood indexes, and diagnostic telemetry are never tracked, aggregated, or shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Conversational Integrity

Your dialogue with the SereneMind Chatbot helper runs over transient sessions with zero-retention parameters, ensuring that the guidance and prompts provided remain completely confidential.
