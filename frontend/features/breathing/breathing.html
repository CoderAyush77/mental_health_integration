How to Start

Follow these simple steps to begin your mindful breathing session:

  1. Select Duration: Choose your preferred time (1, 2, or 5 Minutes) using the dropdown at the top.
  2. Click Start: Press the Start button to begin. You can Pause or Reset at any time.
  3. Synchronize Your Breath: Follow the circle visualizer:
    • Breathe In: Inhale slowly as the circle expands.
    • Hold: Rest your breath gently when the circle stays large and pulses.
    • Breathe Out: Exhale slowly as the circle contracts.
